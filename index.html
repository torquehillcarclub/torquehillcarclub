About TORQUE HILL

Welcome to TORQUE HILL, the premier car enthusiast community in Cameron Highlands. We are a passionate group of automotive lovers who share a common bond - the thrill of the drive and the beauty of the highlands.

Founded by enthusiasts, for enthusiasts, we organize regular meet-ups, scenic drives through the winding mountain roads, and exclusive events that celebrate automotive culture.
